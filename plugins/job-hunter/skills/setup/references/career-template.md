# career.md template

Every fact traces to the resume or an answer. Estimates are `~estimate`. Dates on headings are the day they confirmed the facts. No em dashes, no emoji.

- Header: name, location, contact, links, target title
- Claim boundaries: things never to overstate, in their words, with safe phrasing
- Positioning: one sentence on who they are and what they are best at, plus 3 to 5 keywords employers in their target field use (taken from real postings you searched, not guessed)
- Summary: 3 lines in their words
- Work history, newest first: employer, title, dates, scope, then accomplishments in Action + Metric + Outcome form. Mark estimates `~estimate`. Add "Left off the resume" for anything good they mentioned casually.
- Competency matrix: a table of skill or competency, level (Expert, Working, Exposure), proof point, roles where used, last used. Note gaps against their target titles.
- Data points: every number they gave, one line each, with role, source, and `~estimate` if not exact (volume, scope, before and after, rates)
- Wins: their biggest 3 to 6, each with what changed, the number, and who noticed or what proof exists (award, review, raise)
- Losses and lessons: 2 to 4 failures, mistakes, or hard exits, each with what happened, their part, what they changed, and a calm one-line interview version
- Licenses, certifications, education, with dates
- Story bank: 3 to 5 stories, each situation, task, their action, result, lesson, and tags (leadership, conflict, failure, customer, pressure, change, teaching)
- Open items: anything unknown, with where to find it
