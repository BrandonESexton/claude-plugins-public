How this was scored

Fit score weights

Must-have skills30Hands-on evidence for each must-have requirement.
Relevant experience25Depth, scope, seniority, and recency in comparable work.
Demonstrated impact15Specific, checkable outcomes, not duties.
Learning agility15Picked up new tools, domains, or roles fast and applied them.
Work-style signals5Ownership, collaboration, communication. Weak evidence from paper; probe live.
Nice-to-haves10Preferred skills and domain extras.

Each dimension is scored 1 to 5 with anchors from the rubric, then weighted to 0-100. Advance at 75+ with no must-have missed; Consider at 60+ with at most one missed. A 4 or 5 with no verifiable quote counts as 3.

Integrity tiers

  • High risk: any strong signal (hidden AI instruction, contact details or resume text shared with another applicant, impossible dates, several overlapping full-time jobs) or 3+ moderate. Verify identity before any interview.
  • Verify: any moderate signal or 3+ weak ones. Proceed, and check the specific item.
  • Clear: nothing that needs a separate check.

No single signal proves fraud (FBI, Oct 2026). Signals are prompts to verify, never a reason to reject on their own.

Never scored

  • Name, photo, age or graduation year, gender, race, national origin, citizenship (unless the role legally requires it), religion, disability, family status.
  • School prestige, accent, location (beyond the posting's stated requirement), and non-US education.
  • Employment gaps. They are shown as timeline facts only.

This is decision support, not an automated employment decision. If you hire for NYC roles or use it as the main screen, check Local Law 144 and EEOC guidance with counsel.
