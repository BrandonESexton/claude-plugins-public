# Question bank

Pools, not a script. Pick the questions that fit the gap map, rephrase in your own voice, skip anything the resume already answers. Target 40 to 50 counted questions total.

## Phase 1: Targets and constraints (~6)
- What role or roles do you want next? Name titles, not just a function.
- What level (IC, lead, manager, director) and is that a step up, sideways, or down from now?
- Which industries or company types pull you, and which do you want to avoid? Company size and stage?
- Location and work mode: remote, hybrid, onsite, travel tolerance, relocation?
- Compensation floor and target (skippable). Anything else non-negotiable: benefits, schedule, visa, clearance?
- What do you want to do more of, and what do you want to stop doing?
- Timeline: urgent search or exploring? Currently employed?

## Phase 2: Role deep dives, newest first (~20, scale to the resume)
Per role, in roughly this order:
- Scope: what did you own? Team size, direct reports, budget, users or customers served, systems, geography.
- Why were you hired, and what was broken or missing when you started?
- Biggest accomplishment in this role. What was the before, what did you do (you personally), what was the after?
- The number: how would you measure that result? Time, money, volume, quality, adoption, risk. If no hard number, what is a defensible estimate?
- Second and third accomplishments, same pattern.
- A project you led across teams or without formal authority.
- A tool, process, or automation you built or replaced. What did it replace and what did it save?
- Who did you work with (execs, vendors, customers) and what did they rely on you for?
- Recognition: promotions, awards, a boss or peer who singled you out, performance review language.
- Something you did that is not in your resume bullets.
- Why did you leave (or why are you leaving)? Frame positively but honestly.
Older roles: one question for scope, one for the standout win.

## Phase 3: What is missing (~6)
- Work in the last 6 to 12 months that is not on your resume?
- Side projects, open source, personal builds, home lab, writing, talks, mentoring?
- Certifications, courses, or degrees in progress or not listed?
- Volunteer, board, committee, or community leadership?
- Anything on your resume you would like to downplay? Anything you left off because it felt small?
- Gaps in dates: what were you doing, and what did you learn?
- Tools or technologies you use but never listed.

## Phase 4: Skills with calibration and proof (~6)
- Top 5 skills you want to be hired for. For each: level (Expert = could teach it; Working = used it on real work; Exposure = touched it) and one specific proof.
- Skills a target job would require that you are weakest in. What is your plan or workaround?
- Tools you want recruiters to find by keyword, and one that you do not want to be asked about.
- Leadership and soft skills with evidence: hiring, coaching, conflict, influencing, communicating up.

## Phase 5: Stories (~5)
Ask for the full situation, task, action, result, and what they would do differently.
- A time something you built or decided failed.
- A conflict with a peer, boss, or stakeholder and how it resolved.
- A time you influenced without authority or changed someone's mind with data.
- A time you handled ambiguity or a fast-moving crisis.
- A time you made a build vs buy, cost, or risk tradeoff.

## Phase 6: Drivers and positioning (~4)
- What work energizes you, and what drains you?
- How would your best former manager and best peer describe you in three words?
- What do you want the next employer to know in 30 seconds that a resume does not say?
- What is the story of your career in two sentences? (Then refine it with them.)

## Probing patterns
- "Give me one specific example." "Which customer, system, or project?"
- "What was the number before and after?" "Over what time period?"
- "What would have happened if you had not done it?"
- "You said we. What did you personally do?"
- "How do you know that? Where would that number live?"
- "That sounds like a better bullet than what is on your resume. Say more."
- "On a scale, is that Expert, Working, or Exposure?"
- Offer ranges when stuck; record as `~estimate`.
