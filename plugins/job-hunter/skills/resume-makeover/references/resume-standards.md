# Resume standards

Use as the checklist for the review and the rules for the rewrite.

## Structure (top to bottom)
1. Header: name, city and state, phone, email, one link (LinkedIn or portfolio). In the page body, never in a Word header or footer.
2. Title line and 2 to 4 line summary: target role, years or scope, strongest proof. First person implied, no "I".
3. Core skills: 6 to 10, ordered by what the target role values, calibrated (never list Exposure skills as strengths).
4. Experience, newest first: employer, title, city, dates (month and year), one scope line (team, volume, budget, sites), then 3 to 6 bullets for recent roles, 1 to 3 for older ones.
5. Licenses, certifications, education. Education goes first only for new graduates or when a degree is the gate.
6. Optional: projects, awards, volunteering, only if relevant to the target.

## Bullets
- Action verb + what + how or with what + number + outcome. Vary the opening verbs.
- A number, a named thing, an outcome. If career.md has none, it goes to open items; do not invent.
- No "responsible for", "helped", "assisted", "worked on" when they owned the work. No "we" without their part.
- One idea per bullet, at most two lines.
- Repeat a headline number at most twice on the whole resume.

## Format
- Let the content decide: one page for most people under about 10 years, two pages when the experience is there (two-page resumes do as well as one page in large studies), never three. Never a stranded heading or a one-line orphan at a page break.
- One column, standard font 10 to 12 pt, real bullets, no tables, text boxes, images, icons, or skill bars. Applicant systems read these badly.
- Consistent date style and tense (past for old roles, present for current).
- Save as .docx or PDF with a plain file name: First_Last_Resume.

## Language
- Plain words. No em dashes, no emoji, no buzzwords ("results-driven", "passionate", "synergy", "team player").
- Use the target field's own terms (taken from real postings), but write bullets around what they did. Never copy 8 or more words in a row from a posting.
- Claims never stronger than career.md.

## Red flags to call out
Gaps over 6 months with no explanation, five or more jobs under a year, title that does not match scope, nothing in the last 12 months, outdated tools as the headline, a skills list with no proof, typos, inconsistent dates, personal details that do not belong (age, photo, full street address).
