Senior Platform Engineer
Northwind Logistics (fictional example) | Remote, US | $165,000 - $195,000

About the role
You will join a 6-person platform team that runs the Kubernetes platform behind our routing and billing services (about 180 services, 3 AWS regions). You report to the Director of Infrastructure. Success in year one: self-service environments for product teams, deploy lead time under 30 minutes, and an on-call rotation that pages less than twice a week.

What you'll do
- Own and evolve our EKS clusters, upgrades, and multi-region failover
- Build Terraform modules and golden paths product teams can use without filing tickets
- Run CI/CD (GitHub Actions, Argo CD) and make deploys boring
- Improve observability with Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry
- Lead incident reviews and mentor two mid-level engineers

Requirements
- 5+ years in infrastructure, SRE, or platform engineering
- Production Kubernetes experience (EKS preferred)
- Terraform for real infrastructure, not just tutorials
- Deep AWS experience: networking, IAM, compute
- CI/CD pipeline design (GitHub Actions, Argo CD, or similar)
- Observability: metrics, tracing, alerting (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry)
- Strong Python or Go

Nice to have
- Kafka or another event streaming platform
- Cloud cost optimization (FinOps)
- Security hardening for Kubernetes and IAM
- Logistics or high-volume transactional systems
